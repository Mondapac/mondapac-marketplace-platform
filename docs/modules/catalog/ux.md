## 1. Scope and inventory

Phase 3 UI for `catalog`: **seven seller-panel screens (plus Photos as a section and Import as a reserved page), eight admin-panel screens, thirteen dialogs, fourteen emails.**
**No buyer screen is built here.** The storefront, product page and listing belong to Phase 6; section 3.5 fixes what `catalog`
feeds them and the rules they must follow, so they can't be designed against the grain later. **Not in this module's UI:** price,
special price, cost, stock, "sellable now" (`pricing`, `inventory`, cart: ADR-0024 d5; DD AC 6). The seller's product list and Offer
form reserve slots for them, filled by the client from those modules' own reads, never from `catalog`.

### 1.2 Words (the whole module uses these and no others)
| Word | Means | Never use |
|---|---|---|
| Product | The thing: name, photos, details, sizes | "Item", "SKU" for a product |
| Your product / Shared product | Seller-scoped product / platform-scoped product (seller sees "shared"; admin panels say "Platform product") | "Global", "master" |
| Offer | One seller's listing of one product, with condition, handling, badges | "Listing" for the Offer (the word "Listed" is the status) |
| Size or option; variant (admin only) | One purchasable form of a product | "Variation" |
| Badge | A certification tag on an Offer, built from `evaluateClaims` data | "Verified", "certified" about any product, "approved as {type}" (R13) |
| Live | A published revision | "Approved" for a product (it collides with Seller approved and Certificate approved, CUX 3.0 rule 2) |
| Awaiting review, Changes needed | As SL-UX and CUX | A promised time |
| Listed / Off sale | Catalog's own state (DD 4.4), nothing about price or stock | "Available", "In stock", "Buyable" |
| Showing / Checking / Paused | Badge states (3.3). "Paused" because "Suspended" already names an account state | "Suspended" for a badge |

## 3. Screen specifications

### 3.0 Rules for every screen
1. **Shells.** Seller pages use the full seller shell (the catalogue is not in the limited allow-list, DD 8.2). Admin pages use the admin shell. `Sidebar` and `Topbar` stay instances. Nav items follow IA 3.1 and 3.2: `catalogue` (admin, with any of the four view and edit keys), `s_catalogue` (seller, `catalog.own-product.view`).
2. **Form pages (PS2, PS3, PS5, PS7, PA4 edit, PA5)** follow SL-UX 3.0 rule 2 and 3: error summary takes focus; field errors are icon plus text; primary button `State=Loading` at the same width; lists load as 8 skeleton rows; no page spinner. Form column at most `size/form-max`; PS3 and PA4 add a right column for the status card from 1024 px.
3. **Autosave** (PS3, PA4 platform edit): `FormActionBar` with Autosave on: the status text carries the state; "Save draft" stays as an explicit action. The panel keeps typed text **in memory only**: no localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB or service-worker cache. A reload loses the unsaved text; the saved draft is kept. Reads that carry draft content are `Cache-Control: no-store`.
4. **Market-driven content.** Categories, tax categories (with the official guide link), conditions, family fields, attribute labels, languages, reasons, photo limits and the sensitive-field list arrive as data or translation keys. The frontend has no category, no `halal`, no `GST`, no `AU`, no `AUD`, no type list and no field list. Examples show AU because AU is the launch Market.
5. **Never, on any screen:** a price, a special price, a cost, a stock number, or "buyable" or "sellable" (DD AC 6); the word "verified" or "certified" about a product or seller; a religious ruling or health or allergy advice (R13, R14); a badge built from text, a model, a cached answer or a field the panel guessed; any model sentence as a status, badge text, reason or decision input (R1, R2); a review-time promise; a reviewer name to a seller; a server message string; an original upload (only server-made renditions).
6. **Permissions in the UI** follow ID-UX 3.0 rule 6: no view key, no menu item, B5 on the URL; view without the action, the control is disabled and gives its reason in text, never only a tooltip. The panel renders the **allowed-actions list and denial codes the API supplies** and never infers a permission. Disabled controls stay focusable when they give a reason.
7. **Plain text.** Every text field shows exactly what was typed (ZWNJ and ZWJ are kept). Line under free-text fields: "Plain text only. Web addresses and formatting are shown as typed." Display uses `dir="auto"`, keeps line breaks, never interprets HTML.
8. **Dates and times** use the viewing person's locale. A time that belongs to a seller (submitted, decided, attested, badge changed) is shown in the **seller's work time zone, named**, for the seller and for admins alike (SL-UX 3.0 rule 8). No raw `Date` arithmetic for deadlines.
9. **Scope words.** A page for a shared product says "Shared product" with a line "MondaPac manages this product's name, photos and details. You manage your offer." The content cards are read-only for sellers (CAT-43) and the controls are absent, not disabled.
10. **Staff roles.** With `catalog.own-product.view` only, every field is read-only and the page says "You can view products but not change them. Ask your shop owner." With `.edit`, everything except the Import button (`catalog.own-import.run`) and shelf proposals (`catalog.own-category.propose` and SEL-26).
11. **Throttled requests (every list, form and action).** `request.throttled` carries `retryAfterSeconds`. A **list or read** shows an Attention `InfoBanner` above the table, the rows already loaded stay: "Too many requests. Try again in {n} seconds." (`error.request.throttled.retry`, the number comes from the API; the panel doesn't count down by itself and has no automatic retry except autosave); "Try again" is enabled after the user presses it. An **action** (submit, decide, save, delete) keeps the dialog or form open, nothing is sent again by itself, and the button shows the same line beside it. `request.daily-limit-reached` shows "You've reached today's limit for this. Try again later." with no number and no retry button. Both are text, `role="alert"` for actions and `role="status"` for lists, never a toast alone. No page shows a different wording.

## 5. Copy

Keys are `catalog.<surface>.<element>[.<variant>]`, kebab-case, ICU MessageFormat, en-AU spelling. Errors are `catalog.error.<code>`;
unknown codes use `identity.error.unknown`. The design:ux-copy approach: plain words, one idea per sentence, say what happened, what it
means and what to do next; no jargon, no blame, no promise we can't keep; no religious or health claim (R13, R14); legal reads every line
marked (L). Category names, tax category labels, conditions, reasons and type names are data or keys the **Market** owns.
**Words never used:** verified, certified (about a product), approved as halal, guaranteed, compliant, authentic, "usually", "within {time}", "buyable", "in stock".

## 6. Accessibility, responsiveness, RTL and locale

Gate: WCAG 2.2 AA (ID-UX 6; the brief names 2.1 AA as its minimum).
- **Forms.** Real `<form>`; labels visible; optional fields say "(optional)"; the SKU, product code, URL key and numeric attributes are `dir="ltr"`, `autocomplete="off"`; every claim-word error is linked with `aria-describedby`, `aria-invalid` is set, and the error summary (`role="alert"`) takes focus and lists each field with a link. On-blur checks announce once per result (`role="status"`), never per keystroke. 3.3.7: nothing already given is asked again ("Submit again" and "Load latest" keep values).
- **Autosave.** The status text is a `role="status"` region: "Saved, except {field}" and "Not saved" announce at once; "Saved at {time}" announces at most once every 30 seconds so a screen reader isn't flooded; the explicit "Save draft" button always works.
- **Photos.** "Move earlier" and "Move later" are real buttons on every tile; the result announces "Photo moved to position {n} of {total}"; focus stays on the moved tile's button. Each tile has a text name ("Photo 2 of 5, main photo"), alt text for the thumbnail is the description or product name, upload states use `role="status"` once per file. A drag gesture is optional and never the only way.
- **Handling options and attestation.** A `radiogroup` named "What is this offer?"; each option's example is its description (`aria-describedby`). The attestation checkbox is a labelled checkbox inside the form, never auto-focused; its recorded line is text.
- **Badges.** `CertChip` is a button named from its `badge.sr.*` key; the preview text says it is a preview; paused and checking rows state their status in text; the disabled "Add" is absent when not allowed (no focusable dead control); "Sell without this badge" is a button inside its own card with the confirm in `DP4`; in acting-as it is disabled, stays focusable and names `access.acting-as-refused` in text beside it (3.7). The "Request this badge" checkbox (before the first submit) is named by `badges.row.request.sr` and its state is announced when changed.
- **Tree.** WAI-ARIA tree with the keyboard model of 3.2a; selected node announced with its status and count; the actions are ordinary buttons; no drag. `CategoryTree` rows show status as text.
- **Compare views.** `DataRow` Compare is a table with headers "Live", "New version", "Result"; "Changed", "Review needed" and "Removed" are icon plus text; a photo diff names each photo ("Photo 3 was added").
- **Variant rows.** A table on desktop with column headers; on phones each row is a card with a heading "Size {n}"; the Remove button is named "Remove size {label}".
- **Disabled controls** stay focusable when they give a reason (Approve, Submit, row actions); the reason is text beside the control, not a tooltip.
- **Dialogs.** As ID-UX; DP6, DP9 and DP10 keep typed text on an outside click; destructive dialogs open with focus on Cancel.
- **Motion.** Upload progress and "Checking…" are a static icon plus text under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Targets.** 48 px on seller pages (Touch density); 32 px on admin screens with a 24 px hit area for checkboxes. Toasts last at least 6 seconds and never carry the only copy of a reason.
- **Widths.** Smallest width 320 px for PS1 to PS8 and every dialog; design frames 360 and 1280. Sellers photograph and list on phones (brief section 9), so PS2 to PS7 are **phone-first**: one column, sticky `FormActionBar` with a full-width primary button, photo tiles in two columns, `VariantRow` as cards, handling cards stacked, the Badges card full width. The status card moves above the form under 1024 px. PS1 shows a table from 760 px and cards below. PA1, PA3 table from 760 px and cards below; **bulk selection is desktop and tablet only** (below 760 px no checkboxes, as SL-UX). PA2 is two columns from 1024 px; below that the side column drops under the main column and the decision buttons repeat in a sticky bottom bar below 760 px. PA6 is two columns from 1024 px; below that the tree is full width and a selected category opens its details as a full page. **The full seller shell and every admin page on phones wait for D16.**
- **RTL.** Logical properties only; names, descriptions and photo descriptions use `dir="auto"` and keep ZWNJ and ZWJ; SKU, product code, URL key, numbers and dates entered as numbers stay left-to-right inside an RTL page; the category path separator "›" is mirrored by direction, not hard-coded; icons that point mirror (back, chevron; Move earlier and Move later are in reading order and do not mirror as arrows, they are labelled text). Text may grow 40%: buttons and chips wrap, never truncate. No text in images. Locale tabs follow the Market's locale list; labels inside a locale tab use that locale's direction.
- **Persian terms.** Status words in 3.3 take the brief's Persian terms. Persian badge and claim-word strings are separate keys written by a person with legal review, not machine translated; until they exist the Market's default locale is used and nothing appears in a half-translated sentence.
- **Locale and Market.** Nothing is hardcoded to AU, GST or AUD (3.0 rule 4). A second Market (ZZ: one product type plus a fixture Vertical type, another category tree, tax codes, locale set and a smaller photo limit) must render PS3, PA2 and PA8 with no code change: a Storybook or Playwright case per Market fixture (ADR-0003 decision 9). The language `Tab` is hidden when the Market has one locale.
- **Claims.** The only words that state something about certification on these screens are on `CertChip`, `CertDetail`, the Badges card reasons, and the fixed sentences listed in the copy table; they come from keys and structured data (R1).
